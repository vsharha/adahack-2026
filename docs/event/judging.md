# Judging criteria

Verbatim from `reference/slideshow.pdf`, the opening slides of 3 October 2026. The slides present these as the event's criteria. Postcode Lottery's poster gives its own; see [`../postcode/brief.md`](../postcode/brief.md).

> If judges suspect reliance on AI, points may be deducted accordingly. While there is no zero-tolerance policy, the use of AI is discouraged.

| Marking point       | Max score | Guidance                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Teamwork            | 10        | How much did each person contribute? How well did they work together?                                                                                     |
| Originality         | 10        | Was it original? Did they make it their own? Personality?                                                                                                 |
| Theme               | 10        | How well did their project fit the theme of Sustainability?                                                                                               |
| Presentation Skills | 10        | What was their final presentation? Was it concise and informative? Were they confident in their work? How well did they explain how their project worked? |
| Project Quality     | 20        | How good was their overall project? Was their idea good? Think of code and other technical aspects                                                        |
| Total               | 60        |                                                                                                                                                           |

Not in the slides, and reported by the team from organisers on 3 October 2026: judges visit teams table by table; a team may present with slides, a video or a live demo; one team may enter more than one challenge.
