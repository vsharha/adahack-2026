# Spectacle presentation

Open [`/presentation`](http://localhost:3000/presentation) on the running Postcode app. It has five slides: introduction, the problem, local information and conditional pledges, a live demo, and next steps. It follows the app’s colours and typography in both themes.

Use the arrow keys, Space or the on-screen arrows to move between slides. Spectacle’s presenter view is available with Alt+P and includes speaker notes. The live app appears on slide four. **Open live app** opens a larger view in another tab and leaves the deck ready to resume. The iframe and that tab share the browser’s demo state.

Reset and rehearse the app before presenting, using [`demo.md`](demo.md). The presentation never resets the app automatically. Allow most of the pitch for the litter-pick flow. The notes give the operator cues, source references and limits on each claim.

**Print / PDF** opens all five slides in Spectacle’s export view. Use the browser’s print dialog, landscape orientation, background graphics enabled and no browser headers or footers. Save as PDF for a static backup. The exported demo slide contains the walkthrough text instead of the interactive app.

The deck describes real saved local context alongside demo residents, activities, suggestions and fictional rewards. It makes no claim of measured environmental savings or funded partner agreements. Production identity and organiser permissions remain future work.
