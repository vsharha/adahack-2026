## Before judges arrive

- Reuse the running app at `http://localhost:3000`. Test in the browser and display size used at the table.
- Open `/reset`, press Reset demo, choose Priya and leave Street at the top. Resetting removes only the browser demonstration state.
- Check the litter pick needs one neighbour. Its held control requires enough pledges and the scheduled time, 3 October 2026 at 11:00 UK time, to have passed.
- Rehearse the whole route twice, including account switching, queue confirmation, redemption and voucher persistence after reload. Reset again afterwards.
- Keep the local backup walkthrough ready. It consists of captured app screens with captions, rather than a continuous recording. Narrate it using the same sequence if the live app fails.
- Each teammate states their actual contribution when asked about teamwork. Do not imply a real partner agreement, measured environmental savings or production identity checks.

## If there is time for questions

Explain the reward safeguard: a trial allowance of 100 points per household per UK calendar month. Confirmations award up to the remaining allowance. Spending cannot reopen it, and unused balances carry forward. Private household habits earn self-reported contribution only.

Show Decline if asked about mistakes: the reason field appears on demand, declined reports award nothing, and the household can report again. Other goals remain available through Street, Groups and Home; avoid taking the main presentation into a second goal.
