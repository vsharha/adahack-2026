## Before judges arrive

- Reuse the app at `http://localhost:3000`. Check the browser and display size used at the table.
- Open `/reset`, press Reset demo, choose Priya and leave Street at the top. Check that the litter pick needs one neighbour and Rewards shows 20 available.
- Attendance reporting requires enough pledges and the activity’s scheduled time, 3 October 2026 at 11:00 UK time, to have passed. There is no separate held control.
- Rehearse the two account switches, confirmation and redemption. Reset afterwards. For a rewards-only demonstration, any household can redeem the repair offer immediately.
- Keep a local captured-screen backup ready. Narrate the same sequence if the live app fails.
- Each teammate states their actual contribution when asked. Residents, activities and vouchers are demo data; do not imply real partner agreements, measured savings or production identity checks.

## Questions

The trial reward allowance is 100 points per household per UK calendar month. Starting demo rewards do not use it. Confirmation awards up to the remaining allowance; spending cannot reopen it and unused balances carry forward. Private habits earn self-reported contribution only.

Decline reveals a reason field. A declined report awards nothing, and the household can report again. Approval requires only one eligible organiser and cannot be repeated for extra points. Other goals remain available through Street, Groups and Home.
