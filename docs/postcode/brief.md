# Postcode Lottery challenge

The brief and judging criteria, verbatim. Sources: `reference/postcode-lottery.pdf` (the sponsor's poster) and `reference/slideshow.pdf` (the opening slides), both handed out on 3 October 2026. Text was extracted from the PDFs; line breaks inside the PDFs' words, such as "postcodelottery.co.u k", are joined.

## Poster

> **GREENER BY POSTCODE:** sustainability where you live
>
> At Postcode Lottery UK you don't play on your own. You play with the neighbours who share your postcode, and when you win, your street wins with you.
>
> People care about their own patch, and keeping it green is something a street can do together in a way nobody manages alone. That's your challenge for today's AdaHack Day, Saturday 3 October.
>
> **THE CHALLENGE**
>
> Design and build something that helps neighbours keep their postcode area green. Show them what's happening where they live: the air, the green space, the local electricity. Then give them a reason to act on it together.
>
> You can find all the causes that our players support at postcodelottery.co.uk/good-causes.
>
> Stretch goal: make it work better the more of the street joins in.
>
> The prize for the entry judged best against the criteria is a £70 Lifestyle gift card for each team member
