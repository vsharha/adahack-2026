### Fire-data feasibility checked on 3 October 2026

The public [NatureScot burn layer](https://services1.arcgis.com/LM9GyVFsughzHdbO/arcgis/rest/services/Scottish_Wildfire_and_Muirburn_Extents/FeatureServer/0) returned 20,587 polygons and 1,163 records with a non-null recorded date. These include muirburn and duplicate/event-fragment possibilities; they are not counts of confirmed wildfires or peatland test events. Date meaning, event type, peat overlap and matched pre-fire observations need validation. The [audit](peatpulse-research-audit.md) records queries and actual samples. The subsequent [data audit](peatpulse-data-audit.md) downloaded 372 high-confidence MODIS raster detections in Highland; these are thermal observations, not distinct wildfire counts.

**Limits:** 291 dated burn records intersect peat and form 172 candidate groups. The initial input audit had no verified wildfire labels; the subsequent outcome join below adds reviewed evidence. ### Local model training: verified 3 October 2026

**Works:** [Training report and reproduction commands](peatpulse-training.md). Three 50-tree Random Forests trained entirely on the local CPU: basic weather, strong weather with CEMS indices, and PeatPulse with radar. They share 32,199 training rows, class/episode weighting and fixed settings. The three fits took 0.87 seconds combined; each saved model is approximately 302 KB. Predictions, daily priority ranks, a raw CEMS FWI reference, comparison tables and a three-fire historical plot are saved locally.

**Measured result:** strong weather and PeatPulse are identical on all 112,995 scored rows; radar contributes no tree splits in this fit. Validation/test have zero eligible satellite positives, so their AUCs remain undefined. None of the methods selected the Glenuig 2023 cell at the fixed one-cell-per-day budget during its eligible pre-event window. Halladale's 130-hour lead is a training-period replay and cannot establish generalisation. No improvement percentage is supported.

**Verification:** all 15 tests pass. Project Ruff checks, formatting and Pyright pass. Reloaded models reproduce the stored prediction/status rows exactly from the input-only table; predictor allowlists, time cutoffs, dataset/code hashes and common candidate populations are checked. The case plot was inspected visually. The complete `pnpm verify:compsoc` gate fails on `map-preview/vendor/leaflet.js` (one lint error, 363 warnings), which belongs to the separate data explorer. No lint rule was disabled.

**GitHub status:** source and training notes are included in the push. Raw downloads, model files and generated predictions remain local and excluded from Git.

### Expanded local training: verified 3 October 2026

**Works:** [Five-fold grouped comparison and saved models](peatpulse-expanded-results.md). All discovered burned cells were retained in a separate Scotland-wide 346-cell panel, 2018–2025. Of 1,011,012 cell-days, 535,473 rows from 342 cells pass input, outcome and timing checks. These contain 218 positives across 119 cells and 25 mapped burn groups, linked into 22 positive validation groups. There are 65 validation groups in total. Radar anomalies use strictly preceding 90-day observations.

**Measured result:** weather + CEMS alerts on 6/25 groups, radar on 3/25 and raw FWI on 2/25, each issuing 14,256 alerts across the five held-out folds. The strongest model's mean fold ROC AUC is 0.815 versus 0.629 for FWI. Its 98.53% ordinary accuracy is below the 99.96% always-no-burn reference. The radar same-weather ranking score is 45.5% versus tied weather's 50%, across 17 groups. No radar improvement is supported. Unknown alert outcomes and false alarms remain substantial.

**Verification:** all 20 tests, Ruff formatting/linting and Pyright pass. Data assertions confirm causal features, unique rows, known outcomes for fitting, both classes in every fold, no linked cell/event group crossing folds, and complete out-of-fold scoring. Reloaded models reproduce probabilities within `1e-12`; the tolerance covers floating-point reduction order. Final models, predictions, dataset examples, event results, paired uncertainty intervals and the chart are saved locally. Inner folds select between two predeclared forest configurations; final refits are excluded from evaluation. The repository verification now passes with cached data and bundled Leaflet assets excluded from source formatting and lint checks.

**GitHub status:** source and experiment notes are included in the push. Downloaded data, fitted models and generated predictions remain local and excluded from Git.
