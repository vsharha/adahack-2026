# CompSoc: product

PeatPulse is a proposed historical comparison of fire prediction on peatland: weather alone versus weather plus additional observations of peat condition. The demonstration measures whether those observations improve warnings about fires that actually occurred. The detailed proposed experiment is in the [plan](peatpulse-plan.md); implementation and measured improvement are outstanding.

## Decisions

- Compare weather-only predictions with predictions using additional peat/environmental observations against the same historical actual-fire outcome, because the user wants to demonstrate whether the extra tracking improves advance fire prediction. **3 October 2026.**
- Organize the plan around the finished deliverable, distinctive contribution and success evidence rather than a sequence of progressively expanded prototypes, so both teammates know the intended submission directly. **3 October 2026.**
