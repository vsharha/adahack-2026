## Brief

> Hoppers Challenge: Carbon Portfolio Diversity
>
> Sponsored by Optiver
>
> A sustainable world is one in which our environmental systems and societies flourish, natural resources
> are able to replenish themselves, and people are able to live healthy and secure lives. The growing
> complexity and scale of scientific computing workflows and AI training pipelines place huge demands on
> energy and other natural resources. Sustainability can be overlooked in favour of technological
> advancements, therefore it is of utmost importance for us to make the effort to consider how we can fit
> sustainability into our own projects.
>
> The Challenge
> Build a carbon credit portfolio that delivers 100,000 of CO2 e on a budget and survives the unexpected.
> Try to minimize carbon emissions.
>
> We have used the UC Berkeley Voluntary Registry Offsets Database with a combination of fake synthetic
> prices and failure probabilities.
>
> Link to created DataBase with the fake data created
>
> How will we judge?
> Marks will be given for the following:
>
> - Teamwork (how much did everyone contribute)
> - Originality
> - Theme (How well does your project fit the theme of Sustainability?)
> - Presentation Skills
> - Project Quality
>
