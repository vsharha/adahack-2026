Which inputs are missing?

${part14}

Radar uses Sentinel-1A only, one descending relative orbit per cell selected using training coverage, median backscatter over mapped peat, and 30m extraction scale. An assumed 48-hour delivery delay is applied; observations older than 18 days are marked stale. Actual historical delivery times are unverified, so these remain hindcast features.

ERA5 and CEMS have a roughly 0.25° grid: nearby 1km cells can share weather values. Radar extraction at 30m is summarized over each 1km peat footprint. The data do not establish 10m fire-prediction accuracy. Freezing, vegetation, standing water and terrain can also alter radar backscatter.
